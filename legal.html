Enabling Legislation

Government Contract Continuity Act, 2023

Act No. 12 of 2023 | Assented: 15 March 2023

Establishes the GovContinuity Bureau as an independent statutory body with powers to register, escrow, certify, and arbitrate government contracts during political transitions.

CBN Regulatory Framework

CBN Circular on Government Contract Escrow

Ref: CBN/GOV/2024/001 | Date: 2 January 2024

Directs all deposit money banks to recognize GovContinuity-certified contracts as eligible collateral for lending and to participate in the Bureau's escrow framework.
