Our Mission

Four Pillars of Contract Continuity

1

Register

Every government contract above ₦100M must be registered with the Bureau. We maintain the definitive legal record of obligations, parties, and payment terms.

2

Escrow

Contract funds are placed in CBN-regulated escrow accounts. Disbursements are made upon verified milestone completion — not political convenience.

3

Insure

Political risk insurance guarantees payment even if a successor government attempts cancellation. Contractors and banks are protected against transition risk.

4

Certify

GovContinuity-certified contracts are recognized by courts, banks, and auditors. Certification is prima facie evidence of a valid, binding government obligation.
