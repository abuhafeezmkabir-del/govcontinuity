Protocol

The GovContinuity Protocol

The international standard for government contract continuity verification and arbitration.

3-Factor Verification System

Every contract certified by the Bureau undergoes rigorous three-factor verification before receiving the GovContinuity Seal.

1

Legal Authentication

Verification of appropriation by National Assembly
Due process certification by BPP
Attorney General legal opinion confirmation
Contract validity under Nigerian law
2

Financial Verification

Escrow account establishment with CBN-regulated bank
Verification of fund allocation and availability
Payment milestone schedule validation
Political risk insurance underwriting confirmation
3

Technical Validation

Contractor pre-qualification verification
Scope of work and deliverable definition
Timeline and milestone feasibility assessment
Independent engineer/consultant sign-off

Certification Process

1

Contract Submission

Government or contractor submits contract for registration with all supporting documents.

2

Three-Factor Verification

Legal, financial, and technical verification conducted within 10 business days.

3

Escrow Establishment

Contract funds deposited in CBN-regulated escrow account. Payment milestones registered.

4

GovContinuity Certification Issued

Contract receives the GovContinuity Seal — recognized by courts, banks, and auditors as prima facie evidence of a valid, binding, and funded government obligation.

Arbitration Framework

CBN-Recognized Arbitration

The Bureau serves as the primary arbitration body for contract disputes arising from government transitions. Our rulings are enforceable through the Federal High Court.

Binding arbitration
30-day resolution window
CBN enforcement mechanism
No court appeals on findings of fact
