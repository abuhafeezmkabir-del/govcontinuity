The Crisis

The ₦8.4 Trillion Problem

Every election cycle, Nigeria loses an estimated ₦8.4 trillion in abandoned, renegotiated, or cancelled government contracts. This breaks contractors, destroys bank balance sheets, and paralyzes governance.

Contractors go bankrupt

Unpaid for completed work after government changes

Banks accumulate NPLs

Non-performing loans from contractor defaults

New governments face lawsuits

Inheriting liabilities from previous administrations

Projects like this are at risk during government transitions

The Solution

How GovContinuity Works

STEP 1

REGISTER

Government contracts are registered with full terms, parties, and payment schedules.

STEP 2

ESCROW

Funds placed in CBN-regulated escrow accounts, released upon verified milestones.

STEP 3

INSURE

Political risk insurance guarantees payment even during government changes.

STEP 4

CERTIFY

Contracts receive GovContinuity certification — recognized by courts and banks.
